# Code of Conduct

We follow the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Be respectful, assume good faith, welcome newcomers (many of our users are volunteers new to grant work), and don't harass anyone. Maintainers may remove content or contributors that violate this. Report concerns via the repository's Security/contact channel.
